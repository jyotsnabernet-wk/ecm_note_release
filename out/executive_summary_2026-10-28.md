# DnA Release — October 22-28, 2026 | Executive Summary

- **Retention Reporting** — Finance now sees retention results that exclude Capital Markets, matching the agreed standard reporting scope.
- **Renewals Reporting** — Transfers of solutions between related accounts are no longer miscounted as churn, giving a truer view of renewals.
- **Product Usage Analytics** — Product teams get consistent usage data across all sources, making cross-product analysis simpler and more reliable.
- **Solution Category Reporting** — GTM teams see ESG and Sustainability combined into one category, simplifying filtering and reporting.
- **Partner Performance** — Partnerships teams can now trust the new dashboard's bookings and pipeline metrics to match the prior dashboard's rules.
- **Canada Data Coverage** — Canadian environment data, including history, now flows through the secure data pipeline for consistent reporting.

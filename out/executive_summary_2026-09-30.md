# DnA Release — September 24-30, 2026 | Executive Summary

- **ECM Data Freshness** — ECM reporting data now refreshes every two hours, reducing Finance and GTM data lag from 24 hours to near real-time.
- **ECM Bookings** — Bookings metrics now reflect only realized revenue and include historical ParsePort deals for complete company-wide Finance reporting.
- **ECM Sales & Renewals** — Automated sales hierarchy eliminates manual team-mapping errors; renewal tracking now correctly captures mid-cycle and at-risk ARR.
- **ECM Contracts** — Contract details are now available within bookings and opportunity reporting, giving Finance and GTM richer deal-level context.
- **Account Intelligence** — Account records now include industry classification and private-account status, improving GTM segmentation and reducing manual workarounds.
- **Customer Success** — New partner implementation survey responses from Gainsight are now available for post-partner reporting and trend analysis.
- **Data Governance** — FedRAMP-sensitive organizational data is now properly filtered and prevented from surfacing in downstream analytics outputs.
- **Analytics Tooling** — Jira project and issue data is now available in the data warehouse, enabling engineering and operations reporting.

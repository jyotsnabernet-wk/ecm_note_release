Release Notification

ECM retention &amp; solution category, Partner dashboard fields, FedRAMP prod-CA, User analytics contract

What is happening

This week's DnA release covers the following Analytics Engineering stories.

  • ECM — Capital Markets excluded from retention
    • Capital Markets ARR is removed from account-level and solution-level retention calculations because it is out of scope for standard retention reporting.
    • fct_solution_renewals already excludes Capital Markets via is_metric_solution_renewal, with rows retained for drill-down.
    • fct_solution_retention and fct_account_retention previously still included Capital Markets ARR and are aligned to the same exclusion. (DNA-6327)
  • ECM — combined ESG & Sustainability solution category
    • Users saw ESG and Sustainability as separate Solution Category filter values, while the business wants a single combined value for reporting and filtering.
    • A canonical combined label is applied in the OBT / dimension layer, including OBT_BOOKINGS and OBT_PIPELINE. (DNA-5806)
  • Partnerships & Alliances — opportunity-level partner relationship field
    • Q2 2026 KPIs on the new Partnerships & Alliances Dashboard did not match the old dashboard for Sourced + Direct % of Bookings, Win Rate, Partner Delivery %, and ADS.
    • The mismatch traced to scope differences, including the reporting classification: the old dashboard used a New Logo + New Solution slice, while the new one used all reporting types unless filtered.
    • An opportunity-level partner relationship field is added to OBT_PARTNER_BOOKINGS and OBT_PARTNER_PIPELINE so the new dashboard can apply the old dashboard's rules. (DNA-5821)
  • FedRAMP — prod-Canada sanitization pipeline
    • The GES pre-format Glue jobs are updated to include the new prod-Canada app spot, and history for that app spot is backfilled.
    • Success means prod-ca data flows as expected through the sanitization pipeline (DNA-6387).
    • Separately, work is in progress to investigate causes of parity failures in FedRAMP sanitization. Trivial causes will be fixed, and others will be ticketed with documented fixes. (DNA-6368)
  • User analytics — shared product event contract
    • Work is in progress to align the int_user_analytics_* models to a shared product event contract, including adding source_system.
    • A timebox is looking at silver_prod.user_analytic views that time out because filters are applied after a full table scan, and at the difficulty of analyzing across all event types.
    • Discovery is also underway for SEC Reporting Advanced (Financial Reporting) usage data to assess event tracking and data readiness for future modeling. (DNA-6459 / DNA-6093 / DNA-6365)
  • CData & Customer360 — Silver models and D&B investigation
    • Work is in progress to create CData-sourced models in Silver, aligning with Data Engineering on naming, directory structure, materialization, incremental strategy, metadata columns, testing, and governance tagging for financial data.
    • An investigation is examining the D&B exceptions table and how it can be used to update the existing D&B models. (DNA-6417 / DNA-5922)

Why it is happening

Reporting accuracy & consistency

  • Capital Markets is out of scope for standard retention reporting, so retention metrics should exclude it consistently across renewals, solution retention, and account retention.
  • Finance and RevOps users need a single ESG & Sustainability filter value, and the new Partnerships & Alliances Dashboard needs to reconcile with the old dashboard's KPIs.

Platform & data foundation

  • FedRAMP coverage must extend to the prod-Canada app spot, with parity issues understood and resolved.
  • A shared product event contract, better-performing user analytics views, and Silver-layer CData and D&B models lay the groundwork for future product and customer analytics.

When it will happen

Deploy on

Consumers relying on any changed columns should validate and update downstream references within 30 days of the deploy (2026-10-28).

Downstream impact

  • Retention reporting that reads fct_solution_retention and fct_account_retention will show lower ARR where Capital Markets was previously included.
  • ECM Solution Category filters built on OBT_BOOKINGS and OBT_PIPELINE will show a single combined ESG & Sustainability value instead of two separate values.
  • The Partnerships & Alliances Dashboard consuming OBT_PARTNER_BOOKINGS and OBT_PARTNER_PIPELINE gains the opportunity-level partner relationship field, which may change filtered KPI values such as Win Rate and Partner Delivery %.

Recommended actions

Verify — Account and solution retention figures in fct_account_retention and fct_solution_retention exclude Capital Markets ARR as expected.

Verify — The ECM Solution Category filter shows one combined ESG & Sustainability value, and Partnerships & Alliances KPIs align with the old dashboard when the partner relationship field is applied.

Release Notification

ECM customer data quality &amp; mapping fixes, User Analytics intermediate layer, Support OBT &amp; Zendesk Chat ingestion

What is happening

This week's DnA release covers the following Analytics Engineering stories.

  • ECM — customer data quality & mapping fixes
    • An investigation into grace period logic found that subscriptions are counted as active only strictly between their start and end dates, likely understating customer counts when accounts experience brief lapses before a backdated renewal or new contract is written. (DNA-5512)
    • A separate investigation revealed that intracompany transfers — subscriptions or contracts moving between sibling accounts under the same ultimate parent — are currently misclassified as Churn at the source and New at the destination, overstating both figures. (DNA-5511)
    • The solution_group mapping logic in the customers model has been updated to align with new Operational Reporting requirements, applying revised CASE logic across the SOLUTION_GROUP and BOOKINGS_CATEGORY fields. (DNA-5522)
  • User Analytics — intermediate layer foundation
    • Phase 1 of the USER_ANALYTICS pipeline introduces a set of per-table intermediate models (int_user_analytics_*) that isolate individual fragmented legacy sources and standardize their schemas into a single clean layer.
    • These models serve as the foundational tier for the upcoming gold fact table fct_user_analytics_events, reducing direct dependencies on legacy sources and improving pipeline maintainability. (DNA-5519)
  • Support — OBT build & Zendesk Chat ingestion
    • Following completion of Support refinement activities, development of the OBT (One Big Table) and associated IS_METRIC fields is underway, implementing SQL aligned to the end view requested by Support stakeholders. (DNA-5504)
    • The Zendesk Chat: Messaging Tickets and Chat Engagement datasets are being ingested into Snowflake to provide channel-specific, granular messaging metrics that are absent from the existing ticket-level data. (DNA-5173)

Why it is happening

ECM data accuracy & operational alignment

  • Current customer models understate active subscription counts and overstate both churn and new-logo figures due to missing grace period logic and misclassified intracompany transfers; the solution_group field additionally required realignment with updated Operational Reporting definitions to preserve consistency across downstream reports.

Platform foundations & new data sources

  • The User Analytics intermediate layer decouples future gold models from fragmented legacy sources, establishing a stable & testable foundation for fct_user_analytics_events, while Zendesk Chat ingestion unlocks granular messaging-channel metrics previously unavailable to Support analytics consumers.

When it will happen

Deploy on

Consumers relying on any changed columns should validate and update downstream references within 30 days of the deploy (2026-07-01).

Downstream impact

  • ECM: consumers of the customers model will observe updated solution_group values effective 2026-07-01; active customer counts and churn/new-logo metrics may shift in subsequent releases once grace-period and intracompany-transfer fixes are promoted.
  • User Analytics: models currently reading legacy sources directly should plan migration to the new int_user_analytics_* intermediate layer once Phase 1 is fully promoted; the gold table fct_user_analytics_events will be built atop these models in a future phase.
  • Support: the new OBT and IS_METRIC fields will become the primary surface for Support stakeholder reporting; Zendesk Chat source tables will be available in Snowflake for downstream Support & CX models once ingestion is complete.

Recommended actions

Verify — After the 2026-07-01 deploy, review customers model outputs to confirm solution_group values align with Operational Reporting expectations (DNA-5522); monitor active-customer counts and churn/new-logo trends as the grace-period (DNA-5512) and intracompany-transfer (DNA-5511) investigations conclude and fixes are applied.

No action required for — User Analytics intermediate layer (DNA-5519) and Zendesk Chat ingestion (DNA-5173) at this time; both are in-progress foundational builds with no breaking changes to existing consumer models in this release. Revisit when Phase 1 promotion and Snowflake source availability are confirmed.

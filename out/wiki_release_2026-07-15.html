Release Notification

Product Usage dimensions, Account Enrichment Reverse ETL, ECM transfer classification, Support OBT, Platform data share

What is happening

This week's DnA release covers the following Analytics Engineering stories.

  • Product Usage — workspace usage fact & user dimension models
    • A new conformed daily usage fact, fct_user_workspace_usage_daily, is being built at the user × workspace × environment × day grain, combining engagement signals from workiva_event_log and CUSTOMER_TIME_IN_APP to enable DAU/WAU-style reporting and cohort analysis including first-seen and days-since-activation.
    • A user workspace membership bridge dimension, dim_user_workspaces, has been created sourced from workiva_workspace_membership (production environments only), establishing a consistent join path alongside dim_workiva_users and dim_workspaces.
    • Together these two models form the foundational layer for product adoption and access reporting. (DNA-5594 / DNA-5560)
  • Account Enrichment — Reverse ETL promotion & data quality refinement
    • The Reverse ETL account enrichment job has been promoted to production following successful sandbox validation (DNA-4779), writing 4 enrichment fields from Snowflake directly into the Account object in Salesforce.
    • Logic governing dim_accounts__enriched has been tightened to suppress low-confidence enrichment overrides: fields now revert to Salesforce values when enrichment confidence is LOW, company type is unknown, enrichment revenue is $0 while Salesforce is non-zero, or SIC Code is 9999 (unknown).
    • Updates applied by the SPP team and tracked in BT_DS_ML_PROD.ACCOUNT_ENRICHMENT.AUDIT_LOG_UPDATE are also incorporated to keep the enriched dimension current. (DNA-5268 / DNA-5603)
  • ECM — Intracompany transfer classification & null solution total fix
    • An investigation into how dbt models misclassify intracompany account transfers has been completed: subscription or contract moves between sibling accounts under the same ultimate parent were incorrectly recorded as Churn at the source and New at the destination, double-counting both metrics.
    • A fallback classification path has been added to dim_opportunity_solution_classifications for pipeline deals that lack solution totals, resolving the majority of null reporting_classification values previously caused by the new logic anchoring exclusively on solution total.
    • (DNA-5511 / DNA-5533)
  • GTM — Bookings classification investigation & VoC data cleanup
    • An investigation into five opportunities incorrectly classified as Reno instead of PI-Comm Ops in obt_bookings has been completed, identifying and resolving the root cause of the misclassification.
    • The AUM field — previously populated by Pitchbook but no longer updated — has been removed from Voice of Customer Reverse ETL tables to eliminate potential confusion for downstream consumers. (DNA-5602 / DNA-5601)
  • Support — OBT & IS_METRIC field build
    • Following the completion of Support data refinement activities, the OBT (one big table) and associated IS_METRIC fields are being built out based on validated SQL for the stakeholder-facing end view.
    • This delivers the core analytical surface for Support reporting in a single conformed layer. (DNA-5504)
  • Platform — Tech team direct data share & model materialization fix
    • A safe, curated Snowflake direct data share dataset has been set up for the Tech team, enabling their MVP work for customer direct data sharing into Wdata tables.
    • The fct_marketing_targets model has been re-materialized as a table (previously a view) to align with the standard materialization pattern across other target models in marts. (DNA-5604 / DNA-5591)

Why it is happening

Product & customer analytics

  • Building fct_user_workspace_usage_daily and dim_user_workspaces closes a foundational gap in self-serve product analytics, providing the trusted grain required for DAU/WAU KPIs, cohort studies, and activation reporting across the customer base.
  • Promoting the Account Enrichment Reverse ETL job to production and tightening quality gates in dim_accounts__enriched ensures downstream GTM teams operate on higher-confidence account data, eliminating stale or incorrectly overridden Salesforce fields.

Revenue integrity & platform reliability

  • Fixing intracompany transfer misclassification in ECM models and resolving null reporting_classification values in pipeline directly improves the accuracy of churn, new-logo, and bookings metrics consumed by Finance & RevOps.
  • Removing the stale AUM field from VoC Reverse ETL tables, correcting the Reno/PI-Comm Ops bookings misclassification in obt_bookings, and converting fct_marketing_targets to a table materialization collectively reduce silent data quality risk for downstream consumers.

When it will happen

Deploy on

Consumers relying on any changed columns should validate and update downstream references within 30 days of the deploy (2026-07-15).

Downstream impact

  • fct_user_workspace_usage_daily and dim_user_workspaces are new models shipping in this release; downstream dashboards or queries built on product usage data should reference these models starting 2026-07-15.
  • dim_accounts__enriched will reflect updated enrichment suppression logic; consumers comparing pre- and post-deploy snapshots may observe field-level changes on accounts where enrichment confidence was previously accepted at LOW or where revenue/SIC signals were overriding valid Salesforce data.
  • dim_opportunity_solution_classifications will no longer produce null reporting_classification for pipeline deals without solution totals; ECM and Finance dashboards should see materially improved classification coverage.
  • obt_bookings classification for the five investigated opportunities has been corrected from Reno to PI-Comm Ops; bookings reports and downstream aggregations will reflect this reclassification post-deploy.
  • Voice of Customer Reverse ETL tables will no longer include the AUM column; any downstream queries or Salesforce reports selecting AUM explicitly must be updated to avoid errors.
  • fct_marketing_targets is now materialized as a table; query performance will improve and no schema or metric changes are required.

Recommended actions

Verify — After deploy on 2026-07-15, confirm row counts and key metrics in fct_user_workspace_usage_daily, dim_user_workspaces, dim_accounts__enriched, dim_opportunity_solution_classifications, and obt_bookings match expected values; validate that the Reverse ETL AUM removal has not broken any Salesforce reports or downstream VoC dashboards that may reference the AUM column.

No action required for the fct_marketing_targets table materialization change or the Tech team Snowflake direct data share — both are infrastructure-only updates with no schema, metric, or interface changes for existing consumers.

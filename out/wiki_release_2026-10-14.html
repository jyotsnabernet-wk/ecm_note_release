Release Notification

ECM ParsePort region routing, renewal reporting updates, FedRAMP parity checks, user analytics and D&amp;B investigations

What is happening

This week's DnA release covers the following Analytics Engineering stories.

  • ECM — ParsePort deals routed to EMEA
    • ParsePort deals are now mapped so their Opportunity Alignment Region is set to EMEA.
    • The change covers historical ParsePort booking records, which are updated to the new region.
    • Integration logic for incoming Salesforce data is also updated, so new ParsePort deals inherit the EMEA assignment automatically. (DNA-6293)
  • ECM — renewal reporting aligned to stakeholder feedback
    • fct_solution_renewals is updated to exclude Capital Markets from the reporting metric. The model itself still includes it.
    • Early renewals now roll into their subscription-end quarter, while outcomes are still resolved at the actual churn or renewal date.
    • Two metric variants are exposed: one that allows growth credit and one capped at 100%. (DNA-6305)
  • FedRAMP — GES parity checks
    • Analysis in Snowflake compares row counts by month between the paired tables to confirm they are 1:1.
    • Where counts differ, the work checks whether duplicates explain the gap. Duplicates are noted without further investigation, and any other missing rows are traced to find where they were lost.
    • This work is still in progress. (DNA-6308)
  • User analytics — view performance timebox
    • Queries against the silver_prod.user_analytic views are timing out because filters are applied after a full table scan.
    • This makes even simple queries expensive and makes analysis across all event types difficult.
    • A timeboxed investigation is under way to find a better approach. (DNA-6093)
  • Customer360 — D&B exceptions table investigation
    • The team is investigating what data the D&B exceptions table holds.
    • The goal is to decide how that data can be used to update the existing D&B models.
    • This work is still in progress. (DNA-5922)

Why it is happening

Reporting accuracy & stakeholder alignment

  • ParsePort deals need a consistent EMEA region so regional bookings reporting is accurate for both historical and new records.
  • Renewal metrics needed to match stakeholder expectations on early renewals, Capital Markets treatment and growth-credit handling.

Platform readiness & data quality

  • Parity checks give confidence that FedRAMP tables match their counterparts, and they surface duplicate or missing rows.
  • The user analytics and D&B investigations aim to cut query cost and make better use of available data in future releases.

When it will happen

Deploy on

Consumers relying on any changed columns should validate and update downstream references within 30 days of the deploy (2026-10-14).

Downstream impact

  • Bookings reporting: the Opportunity Alignment Region field now returns EMEA for all ParsePort deals, both historical and newly synced from Salesforce, so regional rollups shift accordingly.
  • Renewals reporting: fct_solution_renewals now excludes Capital Markets from the reporting metric and assigns early renewals to their subscription-end quarter. It also exposes both a growth-credit metric and a metric capped at 100%.
  • User analytics: no change has shipped yet to the silver_prod.user_analytic views, which are under investigation.

Recommended actions

Verify — ParsePort bookings show Opportunity Alignment Region = EMEA in regional bookings dashboards, for both historical and new deals.

Verify — fct_solution_renewals dashboards use the intended metric variant (growth credit or capped at 100%). Also confirm that Capital Markets exclusion and early-renewal quarter placement match expectations.

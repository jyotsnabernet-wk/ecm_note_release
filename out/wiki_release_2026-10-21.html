Release Notification

Intact EU replication, Customer360 and Support semantic views, Overlord source migration planning, Zendesk Knowledge ingestion

What is happening

This week's DnA release covers the following Analytics Engineering stories.

  • Intact — EU replication go-live readiness
    • Tracks the checklist of items required before EU replication can go live, including handing off Coupa ingestion to a new owner.
    • Includes kicking off the GLDetail historical job, evaluating Snowflake EU replication strategies with cost estimates for two options, and covering hourly jobs. (DNA-6240)
  • Customer360 & Support — Snowflake semantic views
    • Investigates whether a semantic view should be built on the account spine / crosswalk for Customer360.
    • Defines and implements the Signals & Actions tied to the sv_support_ticket semantic view for use with Snowflake Cortex Analyst.
    • Business stakeholders are still aligning on the exact Signals and Actions list in the specification sheet. (DNA-6138 / DNA-6249)
  • Platform — Overlord source migration planning
    • Classifies every Overlord source as standardized or custom and flags sources holding FedRAMP-regulated data, such as Workday. The output feeds the migration wave plan and the FedRAMP handling design.
    • Assesses current Fivetran entitlement and usage: the license, the connectors in use, and spare capacity.
    • Also documents the MAR per-connector cost model, including the 2026 changes where deletes count and each connector has a $5 minimum. (DNA-6053 / DNA-6056)
  • Zendesk — Knowledge Base engagement fields
    • The zendesk.article table exists in Redshift and Snowflake but lacks engagement fields: views, comments, subscriptions and upvotes. Fivetran does not currently pull these fields.
    • The work extends Zendesk article ingestion to include them, so the DX team can see how users engage with knowledge base content. (DNA-5171)

Why it is happening

Platform & migration readiness

  • EU replication and the Overlord migration need clear ownership, cost visibility, source classification and FedRAMP handling before go-live and wave planning can proceed.
  • Understanding Fivetran usage and the MAR cost model helps the team use existing licensed capacity and avoid unexpected connector costs.

Analytics & customer insight

  • Semantic views for Customer360 and Support let Snowflake Cortex Analyst answer business questions consistently from governed definitions.
  • Zendesk engagement fields give the DX team the data to measure and improve knowledge base content.

When it will happen

Deploy on

Consumers relying on any changed columns should validate and update downstream references within 30 days of the deploy (2026-10-21).

Downstream impact

  • Support semantic view: the sv_support_ticket semantic view will gain Signals & Actions once the specification is finalized, which affects Cortex Analyst consumers.
  • Zendesk Knowledge: zendesk.article will gain engagement fields in Snowflake once ingestion is extended, which affects DX reporting that reads from it.
  • Customer360: the account spine / crosswalk models may later be exposed through a semantic view, depending on the investigation outcome.

Recommended actions

Verify — Confirm that the new engagement fields in zendesk.article populate correctly in Snowflake once DNA-5171 is released.

Verify — Review the Signals & Actions definitions for sv_support_ticket with Support stakeholders before relying on them in Cortex Analyst.

Release Notification

Amplitude event taxonomy, Zendesk support data, C360 &amp; ECM customer intelligence, CPX contracts, workforce utilization, platform

What is happening

This week's DnA release covers the following Analytics Engineering stories.

  • Amplitude — curated event taxonomy for product analytics
    • Thousands of distinct event_type values in Amplitude lack consistent categorization, making reliable product-level reporting difficult across teams.
    • A governed event category taxonomy is being established to standardize classification across all product usage events ingested into the warehouse.
    • This taxonomy will power consistent, trustworthy reporting in fct_amplitude_events and all downstream product analytics marts. (DNA-5446)
  • Zendesk — support analytics data enrichment
    • The Solution Offered Timestamp field is being exposed in the Silver layer on the Zendesk Ticket object with a full historical backfill, closing a gap in SLA and resolution-time analysis. (DNA-5489)
    • The Zendesk Voice: Calls dataset is being ingested into Snowflake for the first time, enabling precise call-channel metrics to be analyzed alongside existing ticket data for a complete end-to-end support view. (DNA-5174)
  • C360 & ECM — customer intelligence infrastructure
    • Persisted transactional audit tables are being created in the Gold layer to track historical changes over time, log AI & Rules Engine outputs, and support drift and accuracy analysis by analysts and data scientists. (DNA-5422)
    • The reporting classification dimension is being updated to replace its internal derivation logic with the validated solution motion logic; existing column values and names are preserved to avoid downstream breakage. (DNA-5394)
    • A structured gap analysis is underway comparing current Snowflake customer models against revised specifications prepared externally, producing a prioritized list to guide future ECM model alignment. (DNA-5478)
  • CPX — contract data modernization & Gainsight mapping
    • Contract correction logic is being migrated from Redshift to Snowflake, delivering a continuous snapshot view of pre- and post-QTC contract data and unlocking CPX use cases that previously required Redshift access. (DNA-5108)
    • The dim_cpxdna_gainsight_mapping seed is being updated to reflect the latest approved Gainsight account mappings, ensuring downstream customer success models reflect current account structures. (DNA-5229)
  • Workforce & HoB — utilization models and sales hierarchy
    • New models fct_workers_time_off_daily and fct_workers_availability_quarter are being built as materialized tables to power workforce utilization reporting. (DNA-5383)
    • An intermediate model for the Sales Hierarchy is being created to expose Sales Operations seed data across dbt projects, incorporating versioning, surrogate keys, full documentation, and data tests. (DNA-5430)
  • Platform — Iceberg–Snowflake integration and dbt deprecation cleanup
    • An investigation and initial proof-of-concept is underway to connect Apache Iceberg tables to Snowflake, evaluating catalog options and validating a single end-to-end connection. (DNA-5038)
    • Outstanding dbt deprecation warnings are being resolved to keep the project on supported configurations and reduce accumulated technical debt. (DNA-5130)

Why it is happening

Product & customer analytics

  • Inconsistent event classification in Amplitude undermines product reporting reliability; a governed taxonomy ensures that fct_amplitude_events and downstream marts reflect accurate, comparable usage signals across every product surface.
  • The C360 enrichment audit tables and ECM solution motion logic change strengthen customer intelligence integrity by providing historical lineage and aligning classification dimensions with validated business definitions, reducing analytical error risk.

Data infrastructure & support analytics

  • Ingesting the Zendesk Voice: Calls dataset and backfilling the Solution Offered Timestamp closes key measurement gaps in support analytics, enabling SLA, channel-performance, and end-to-end resolution analysis in a single semantic layer.
  • The Iceberg–Snowflake integration proof-of-concept and dbt deprecation cleanup reduce platform technical debt, advance open-table-format capabilities, and keep the warehouse toolchain on stable, supported versions.

When it will happen

Deploy on

Consumers relying on any changed columns should validate and update downstream references within 30 days of the deploy (2026-06-17).

Downstream impact

  • fct_amplitude_events and any downstream product analytics marts that join on the event_type category field will reflect the new standardized taxonomy once DNA-5446 is deployed — category-level row counts and aggregations should be validated.
  • Models consuming dim_cpxdna_gainsight_mapping should be spot-checked against the approved spreadsheet after the seed refresh (DNA-5229); the new contract correction tables introduced in DNA-5108 add net-new upstream Snowflake objects that CPX reporting models should reference going forward.
  • The reporting classification dimension (DNA-5394) deliberately preserves existing column names and values — no consumer query changes are required, but mart-level aggregations should be spot-checked for any logic-driven count shifts.
  • New models fct_workers_time_off_daily and fct_workers_availability_quarter (DNA-5383) are net-new additions; workforce and utilization report owners should onboard to these tables and confirm grain and date-range coverage meet their needs.
  • The intermediate Sales Hierarchy model (DNA-5430) is a new cross-project reference point; any HoB consumers currently reading sales hierarchy data from seeds directly should migrate references to this intermediate model.
  • Zendesk Silver layer additions (DNA-5489, DNA-5174) are additive — existing ZD-based models are unaffected, but teams building or refreshing support dashboards should plan to incorporate the new Solution Offered Timestamp column and the Voice: Calls dataset.

Recommended actions

Verify — confirm that fct_amplitude_events event-category distributions align with expectations post-deploy (DNA-5446); spot-check dim_cpxdna_gainsight_mapping row totals against the approved Gainsight spreadsheet (DNA-5229); and validate that reporting classification dimension values are unchanged for all existing consumers (DNA-5394 / DNA-5130).

Verify — utilization report owners should confirm fct_workers_time_off_daily and fct_workers_availability_quarter are fully populated and accessible after the deploy (DNA-5383); HoB stakeholders should confirm the new intermediate Sales Hierarchy model is referenced in place of any direct seed reads (DNA-5430).

No action required for — the Zendesk Silver layer additions (DNA-5489, DNA-5174), C360 enrichment audit tables (DNA-5422), ECM customer model gap analysis (DNA-5478), Iceberg proof-of-concept (DNA-5038), and dbt deprecation cleanup (DNA-5130), as these are additive or internal changes with no breaking impact on existing consumers.

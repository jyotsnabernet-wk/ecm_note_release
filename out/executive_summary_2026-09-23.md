# DnA Release — September 17-23, 2026 | Executive Summary

- **Revenue & Bookings** — Bookings now include Parseport deals, PI deal classification, contract details, and corrected renewal dates for accurate revenue tracking.
- **Renewals Accuracy** — False renewal events from inactive contracts removed, eliminating inflated renewal forecasts and restoring correct ARR visibility for Finance.
- **GTM & Pipeline** — Pipeline reporting now aligns with Marketing definitions, closing opportunity coverage gaps in qualified pipeline data from 2023 onward.
- **Customer & Account Data** — Private account identification and Salesforce account hierarchy mappings are now available, improving account segmentation and legal reporting.
- **Product & Compliance Reporting** — GRC platform upgrade status and CSRD sustainability customer flags are now accessible, supporting compliance and product transition tracking.
- **Customer 360** — Account crosswalk mappings validated, confirming data linkage accuracy across customer records for downstream reporting.
- **Data Infrastructure** — New external data ingestion capability established, enabling broader and more reliable data sourcing into the analytics platform.

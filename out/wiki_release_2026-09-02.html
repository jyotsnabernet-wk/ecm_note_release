Release Notification

ECM Renewal model, Pipeline filter alignment, Orchestration migration assessment, C360 DUNS monitoring

What is happening

This week's DnA release covers the following Analytics Engineering stories.

  • ECM — Renewal model enhancements
    • The fct_solution_renewals model is updated to evaluate each renewal event using a grace period of 90 days after a contract's subscription_end_date, replacing the previous first-day-of-quarter evaluation point.
    • Intra-company transfers are now included in renewal event logic, broadening the scope of renewal reporting for Finance and RevOps. (DNA-5998)
  • ECM — Pipeline filter alignment with L2C changes
    • The Salesforce opportunity field first_year_s_s_amt has been deprecated by L2C platform changes; fct_pipeline is being updated to exclude opportunities based on the summed solution total pipeline amount instead.
    • fct_qualified_pipeline_generated is being aligned with the same exclusion logic already in fct_pipeline and obt_pipeline, replacing the legacy $0 S&S filter inherited from Redshift.
    • New-customer opportunities are being corrected so that int_reporting_classification uses summed solution totals rather than the deprecated S&S field for NL classification, and the model's dependency on fct_bookings is being removed. (DNA-6082 / DNA-6034 / DNA-6004)
  • Orchestration migration — Overlord & Airflow assessment
    • Specialty orchestration behaviours in Overlord (dynamic generation, bespoke interval handling) are being documented to ensure nothing is lost during migration to Airflow or an ingestion tool.
    • The current MWAA deployment is being audited for sizing, version, and configuration, with the Airflow 2.x to 3.0 breaking-change impact captured for migration planning.
    • Every Overlord data source is being classified as standardized or custom and FedRAMP-regulated sources are being flagged to drive the wave plan and compliance design. (DNA-6055 / DNA-6054 / DNA-6053)
  • Data infrastructure — Fivetran assessment & Edgar data share
    • The current Fivetran license, active connectors, and spare capacity are being documented to understand under-leveraged entitlement and the updated 2026 MAR cost model (deletes now count; $5/connector minimum).
    • Data share infrastructure is being created to bring Edgar / SEC Filings data into the warehouse, enabling new analytical use cases. (DNA-6056 / DNA-5739)
  • Customer 360 — DUNS number monitoring
    • A Snowflake query is being developed to identify all existing, new, and deprecated DUNS numbers monitored via the D&B data share.
    • The output will surface numbers that should be added or removed from monitoring, establishing a repeatable process for DUNS lifecycle management. (DNA-6038)
  • DX — Zendesk Knowledge Base engagement ingestion
    • The Zendesk article ingestion is being extended to include engagement fields (views, comments, subscriptions, upvotes) currently absent from the zendesk.article table in Snowflake.
    • These fields will enable the DX team to measure how users interact with knowledge base content and inform content strategy. (DNA-5171)

Why it is happening

ECM model accuracy

  • L2C platform changes deprecated the Salesforce X1st_Year_S_S__c opportunity field, making existing pipeline and classification logic unreliable; affected models must migrate to summed solution totals to preserve accurate revenue reporting.
  • The renewals model lacked a grace period and excluded intra-company transfers, causing systematic under-counting of renewal events and distorting renewal rate metrics for Finance and RevOps.

Platform & infrastructure modernisation

  • Planned migration away from Overlord requires up-front documentation of bespoke behaviours, source classification, and tooling coverage gaps to avoid data loss and ensure orchestration continuity.
  • Fivetran under-utilisation review and the Edgar data share onboarding reflect broader efforts to rationalise ingestion costs and expand analytical data coverage ahead of the next planning cycle.

When it will happen

Deploy on

Consumers relying on any changed columns should validate and update downstream references within 30 days of the deploy (2026-09-02).

Downstream impact

  • fct_solution_renewals — updated grace-period and intra-company transfer logic will change renewal event counts and coverage; downstream renewal rate metrics and Finance dashboards should be validated against pre-release baselines.
  • fct_pipeline and obt_pipeline — removal of the first_year_s_s_amt filter may shift included opportunity sets and alter pipeline totals in GTM and Finance reports.
  • fct_qualified_pipeline_generated — realigned $0 exclusion logic may change qualified pipeline figures surfaced in GTM dashboards.
  • int_reporting_classification and dim_opportunity_solution_classifications — corrected NL classification for new-customer opportunities will affect ECM-derived booking and classification outputs downstream.

Recommended actions

Verify fct_solution_renewals renewal counts and rates post-deploy, confirming grace-period evaluations and intra-company transfers appear correctly in renewal dashboards.

Verify fct_pipeline, obt_pipeline, and fct_qualified_pipeline_generated totals against pre-release baselines to confirm the S&S filter replacement produces expected pipeline figures.

Verify int_reporting_classification NL flags for new-customer opportunities are correct and that no unexpected reclassifications appear in ECM booking and classification reports.

Release Notification

ECM model accuracy, CPX dimension enrichment, Amplitude event taxonomy, workspace dimension, platform hygiene

What is happening

This week's DnA release covers the following Analytics Engineering stories.

  • ECM — Customer model accuracy & hierarchy fixes
    • Intracompany transfers — where a subscription or contract moves between sibling accounts under the same ultimate parent — are being corrected so that customer model views no longer misclassify these movements as Churn and New, eliminating the resulting double-count in churn and new-logo figures.
    • Low-lift corrections are being applied to customer logic, including excluding $0 ARR accounts from customer status and incorporating Parseport and UP account adjustments identified during the gap analysis between the authoritative customer data and current dbt models.
    • The owner role filter in the ECM role alignment hierarchy has been corrected after investigation revealed discrepancies between the legacy Redshift and new Snowflake dashboards at each hierarchy level. (DNA-5511 / DNA-5510 / DNA-5468)
  • CPX — Dimension model enrichment & data ingestion
    • The dim_accounts model is being extended with additional Salesforce Account fields requested by CPX, including Support_Tier__c, ESG_Segment__c, CPX_Segment__c, High_Impact_Customer__c, CurrencyIsoCode, and SS_Amount__c converted to USD.
    • The dim_solutions model is being extended with five new CPX-requested fields: SS_Amount__c (USD-converted), Churned_Effective_Date__c, Preferred_Language__c, CSA__c, and CSA_Manager__c.
    • Additional CPX source fields are being ingested per the CPX Data Ingestion 2 specification, and the dim_cpxdna_gainsight_mapping seed file has been updated and expanded with new Gainsight mapping rows. (DNA-5500 / DNA-5501 / DNA-5505 / DNA-5229)
  • Workspace & VoC — Platform dimension and survey enrichment
    • A new marts-layer dimension table, dim_workspaces, is being created as a single source of truth for Workiva workspaces, resolving the associated Salesforce account for each workspace via a join to dim_accounts.
    • A workspace_id column is being added to the Voice of Customer survey responses by joining walkme_survey_analysis_export with workiva_workspace on the analytics_property_type key, enabling workspace-level segmentation of survey data. (DNA-5499 / DNA-5508)
  • Amplitude — Curated event taxonomy for product analytics
    • Amplitude's native Event Category field is inconsistently populated across thousands of distinct event_type values; developer-specific and missing labels make the field unreliable for product reporting.
    • This release establishes a curated event category taxonomy surfaced in fct_amplitude_events and downstream marts, replacing ad-hoc labels with standardized, reporting-ready categories for consistent product usage analysis. (DNA-5446)
  • Account Enrichment — SPP data ingestion & pipeline monitoring
    • The SPP Jira project data (confirmed free of customer PII by the SPP team) is being consumed into Snowflake and exposed via a new Silver model to support LLM-based account enrichment workflows.
    • End-to-end pipeline failure alerting has been configured for all Account Enrichment Snowflake tasks; on task failure, error events are captured via TASK_HISTORY and routed to Data Ops for self-service monitoring without engineering intervention. (DNA-5513 / DNA-5481)
  • Support — OBT & IS_METRIC field build-out
    • Following refinement of Support analytics requirements, the OBT (One Big Table) and IS_METRIC fields are being built out based on the finalized SQL for the stakeholder-facing end view, enabling self-service Support metrics for downstream consumers. (DNA-5504)
  • Data Platform — Iceberg integration, dbt cleanup & test hygiene
    • An Apache Iceberg table connection to Snowflake has been successfully tested, establishing the catalog approach for future iceberg-backed datasets.
    • Several dbt schema YAML files containing model-name typos and other configuration errors causing dbt run failures have been corrected.
    • Freshness checks on the Pitchbook test data feed have been removed from dbt, eliminating stale-data test failures for a data source that is no longer being pursued. (DNA-5038 / DNA-5479 / DNA-5506)

Why it is happening

Customer & revenue analytics

  • ECM model corrections eliminate double-counting of churn and new-logo figures caused by intracompany account transfers, improve alignment with finance-authoritative customer counts by excluding $0 ARR accounts, and restore accurate role-hierarchy filtering for regional reporting.
  • CPX dimension enrichment in dim_accounts and dim_solutions unblocks CPX team reporting on support tier, ESG & CPX segment, CSA ownership, and currency-adjusted revenue — fields previously unavailable in the marts layer.

Platform quality & operational readiness

  • The new dim_workspaces table provides a canonical workspace-to-account join key, reducing ad-hoc joins and improving consistency across platform analytics workloads.
  • A curated Amplitude event taxonomy removes reporting inconsistency introduced by thousands of inconsistently categorized product events, enabling reliable product usage metrics.
  • Pipeline alerting for Account Enrichment, dbt YAML cleanup, and removal of stale Pitchbook checks reduce operational noise and enable Data Ops self-service monitoring.

When it will happen

Deploy on

Consumers relying on any changed columns should validate and update downstream references within 30 days of the deploy (2026-06-24).

Downstream impact

  • fct_amplitude_events and downstream product analytics marts will reflect the new curated event_category taxonomy; any reports filtering on event category should be validated post-deploy.
  • dim_accounts gains new CPX-requested Salesforce fields including SS_Amount__c (USD) and CPX_Segment__c; downstream models joining on this dimension should confirm no schema-breaking changes.
  • dim_solutions is extended with five new CPX fields including Churned_Effective_Date__c and CSA__c; CPX reporting layers consuming this model should verify field availability.
  • dim_workspaces is a net-new marts table; event-level models that previously performed ad-hoc workspace joins should be updated to reference this canonical dimension.
  • ECM customer model views governing churn, new-logo, and customer status logic have been updated; revenue and retention dashboards sourced from these views should be re-validated.
  • The dim_cpxdna_gainsight_mapping seed has been updated with new mapping rows; Gainsight-linked reports should confirm mapping accuracy post-deploy.
  • The Support OBT and IS_METRIC fields are net-new additions; dependent stakeholder end-views will be available after deploy on 2026-06-24.
  • The new SPP Silver model in Snowflake is net-new; LLM account enrichment pipeline consumers can begin referencing it after deploy.

Recommended actions

Verify — Re-run ECM churn and new-logo reports after deploy on 2026-06-24 to confirm intracompany transfer movements are correctly classified and that $0 ARR accounts are excluded from customer counts; validate role-hierarchy filters for regional breakdowns in the ECM dashboard.

Verify — Confirm dim_accounts and dim_solutions expose all newly requested CPX fields and that downstream CPX & Gainsight-linked reports surface correct values; spot-check fct_amplitude_events event category labels against the new taxonomy.

No action required for dim_workspaces creation, Account Enrichment alerting, Iceberg connection, dbt YAML cleanup, or Pitchbook test removal — these are either net-new additions or internal infrastructure changes with no breaking impact on existing consumers.

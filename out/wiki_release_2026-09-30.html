Release Notification

ECM Bookings Accuracy &amp; Freshness, FedRAMP Test Fix, EDGAR Refinement, C360 D&amp;B, Platform Enablement

What is happening

This week's DnA release covers the following Analytics Engineering stories.

  • ECM — Bookings data completeness & accuracy
    • Historical ParsePort bookings, previously held in a standalone Finance table, are being unioned into obt_bookings to support total company-level bookings analysis across all booking sources.
    • The is_metric flag logic in obt_bookings is being updated to exclude future-dated records, ensuring metrics reflect only realized, historical bookings rather than anticipated ones.
    • Historical New Logo (NL) opportunities are being reclassified by applying pre-QTC conditional logic, restoring accuracy to segment breakdowns that were previously masking NLs as NS. (DNA-6211 / DNA-6210 / DNA-6204)
  • ECM — Freshness cadence, sales hierarchy & solution discrepancies
    • A dedicated 2-hour refresh cadence is being implemented for ECM Gold models, cutting data lag from up to 24 hours and discouraging direct queries against ungoverned Silver layers.
    • Sales hierarchy mapping is being automated to replace manual overrides in gold_prod.bi.team_mapping, eliminating null team and region rows that currently cause reporting failures in QuickSight attainment datasets.
    • Discrepancies in solution category allocations within customer models are under investigation, with findings expected to surface Salesforce data quality issues affecting the solution hierarchy. (DNA-5617 / DNA-5893 / DNA-6159)
  • FedRAMP & Amplitude — Compliance sanitization
    • The broken test assert_workiva_ges_script_run_operation_event_no_fedramp_sensitive_leakage is being fixed to accurately detect sensitive data leakage rather than falsely flagging records with no relevant columns.
    • DBT sanitization models are being built in the Athena project to produce compliant Iceberg table outputs from Amplitude data, with dedicated unit tests for each sanitization macro. (DNA-6254 / DNA-5945)
  • Customer 360 — D&B intelligence & monitoring
    • The D&B exceptions table is being investigated to determine how its data can enrich and update existing D&B models powering account intelligence.
    • A structured API client and registration strategy for D&B Monitoring+ is being designed to support programmatic DUNS number management and automated alert workflows. (DNA-5922 / DNA-6044)
  • EDGAR — Data model refinement
    • The EDGAR data model is being scoped for refinement; at minimum, account prioritization scoring requires ingestion of '33 Act registration filings (S-1, S-4, 424B) as a prerequisite dependency. (DNA-6232)
  • Platform — Semantic views, user analytics & OpenFlow
    • Semantic View support is being added in Snowflake to expose governed, business-friendly query surfaces for downstream consumers.
    • User analytics views in silver_prod.user_analytic are being investigated to resolve query timeout issues caused by post-scan filtering applied across full table scans.
    • OpenFlow environment prerequisites — including Terms of Service acceptance and runtime setup — are being prepared ahead of a planned proof-of-concept session. (DNA-6208 / DNA-6093 / DNA-6228)

Why it is happening

ECM data quality & reporting fidelity

  • Finance and RevOps depend on obt_bookings for pipeline and attainment reporting; incomplete historical data, future-dated metric flags, and misclassified NLs all distort those outputs and erode trust in governed models.
  • Stale ECM Gold models — lagging up to 24 hours behind Salesforce Silver — push analysts toward ungoverned Silver queries, creating metric inconsistency and increased compute risk.

Compliance, platform reliability & intelligence expansion

  • A falsely-triggering FedRAMP leakage test generates compliance noise and must be corrected before Amplitude sanitization models can safely move to production.
  • Expanding D&B and EDGAR intelligence supports the account prioritization roadmap, while platform improvements such as semantic views and user analytics optimization reduce engineering toil and improve self-serve reliability.

When it will happen

Deploy on

Consumers relying on any changed columns should validate and update downstream references within 30 days of the deploy (2026-09-30).

Downstream impact

  • obt_bookings will reflect the full ParsePort historical bookings universe and will enforce future-date exclusion on all is_metric flags — downstream Finance aggregations and QuickSight pipeline reports should be validated for row counts and metric totals.
  • fct_targets_spp and QuickSight ECM attainment datasets will benefit from automated sales hierarchy mapping, removing null team and region rows previously caused by missing manual overrides in gold_prod.bi.team_mapping.
  • ECM Gold models will refresh on a 2-hour cadence, reducing report staleness and decreasing ad-hoc direct queries against Silver layers by downstream BI consumers.
  • Amplitude Athena Iceberg tables (sanitized DBT outputs) will become the compliant source for federal-adjacent analytics consumers once sanitization models are fully deployed and tested.
  • silver_prod.user_analytic views are under active optimization; downstream consumers should expect improved query performance and reduced timeout failures once restructuring is complete.

Release Notification

ECM customer data integrity, FedRAMP access governance, orchestration migration readiness

What is happening

This week's DnA release covers the following Analytics Engineering stories.

  • ECM — subscription data quality & cycle time metrics
    • The internal WF Test account (0014000000YgdVaAAJ) was incorrectly passing through filters and appearing in customer-facing subscription models; the fix enforces the is_test_or_internal_account exclusion logic across fct_subscriptions_effective_daily and all downstream models.
    • New cycle time metric flags are being added to obt_bookings to enable downstream metric tracking at configurable dimension granularities such as solution_group and bookings_category; dbt schema documentation will be updated to reflect the new flag definitions.
    • An ECM model deep-dive preparation is also underway to inform upcoming architectural improvements. (DNA-6279 / DNA-6209 / DNA-6286)
  • FedRAMP — access governance & data parity
    • The APP_INGESTION_* role is being granted equivalent privileges on Iceberg tables as it holds for regular tables, views, and other objects, closing an access gap in the FedRAMP sanitized environment.
    • A partition column gap in Workiva source tables is being resolved by switching from the timestamp column to partition_0 on Glue-defined tables, restoring complete dataset retrieval on full refreshes.
    • Row-count parity checks between production and FedRAMP replicas for Wdesk Classic tables are in progress to validate completeness and identify any missing rows. (DNA-6296 / DNA-6301 / DNA-6291)
  • Platform — orchestration migration readiness
    • Specialty behaviors in Overlord — including dynamic DAG generation and bespoke interval handling — have been identified, mapped to target-tool coverage, and flagged as migration gaps where no equivalent exists.
    • The managed Airflow (MWAA) deployment has been audited for sizing, version, and configuration, with Airflow 2.x to 3.0 breaking-change impact documented to support migration planning.
    • Every Overlord source is being classified as standardized or custom with FedRAMP-relevant sources flagged to drive the wave plan; Fivetran license, connector usage, and MAR cost model are being assessed in parallel to inform replatforming decisions. (DNA-6055 / DNA-6054 / DNA-6053 / DNA-6056)
  • Customer 360 & Semantic Views — account spine exploration
    • A semantic view approach is being evaluated for the account spine / crosswalk to improve Customer 360 flexibility and reusability across analytics consumers.
    • A semantic view for Survey data is under investigation to standardize access patterns; a design worksheet has been shared for stakeholder review. (DNA-6138 / DNA-6294)
  • EU Replication & Zendesk Ingestion — data availability
    • The Intact EU replication go-live checklist is being completed, covering Coupa ingestion handoff, GL Detail historical job, Snowflake EU replication strategy selection, and hourly job configuration.
    • Zendesk article ingestion is being extended to include knowledge base engagement fields (views, comments, subscriptions, upvotes) currently absent from the Fivetran pull, enabling DX team content analytics. (DNA-6240 / DNA-5171)

Why it is happening

Data quality & governance

  • Allowing an internal test account to persist in customer models risks overstating subscription counts and distorting Finance and RevOps pipeline reporting; the fix enforces the intended exclusion logic across the full subscription model chain.
  • Extending FedRAMP Iceberg grants and correcting Workiva partition gaps ensures regulated-environment consumers have complete, consistent access equivalent to the standard production environment.

Platform migration readiness

  • Documenting Overlord specialty behaviors and auditing MWAA prevents institutional knowledge loss and surfaces breaking-change risks before orchestration migration begins, ensuring nothing bespoke is silently dropped.
  • Classifying all sources and assessing Fivetran capacity provides the factual basis for wave sequencing and cost-optimized replatforming decisions across the data platform.

When it will happen

Deploy on

Consumers relying on any changed columns should validate and update downstream references within 30 days of the deploy (2026-10-07).

Downstream impact

  • fct_subscriptions_effective_daily and all models downstream of it will reflect corrected customer-only data after the WF Test exclusion fix is deployed; validate row counts and subscription totals in dependent reporting layers.
  • obt_bookings will gain new cycle time flag columns; downstream models and dashboards consuming this table should be reviewed after the field additions are released to confirm schema compatibility.
  • FedRAMP-environment consumers of Iceberg tables exposed via the APP_INGESTION_* role will regain full read access; Workiva-sourced models rebuilt on partition_0 will return complete row sets on the next full refresh run.

Recommended actions

Verify — confirm that WF Test (0014000000YgdVaAAJ) no longer appears in fct_subscriptions_effective_daily or any downstream subscription models after deploy on 2026-10-07.

Verify — validate that APP_INGESTION_* role holders can query all Iceberg tables in the FedRAMP sanitized environment after the updated grants are applied.

Verify — confirm that full-refresh runs on Workiva Glue-defined tables return complete, gap-free row counts once the partition_0 column switch is deployed.

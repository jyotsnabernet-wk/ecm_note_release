Release Notification

Partner Dashboard Support, ECM Customer Model Accuracy, Customer360 D&B Hierarchy

What is happening

This week's DnA release covers the following Analytics Engineering stories.

  • Partner Dashboard — managed service flag enhancement
    • To support ongoing partner dashboard build-outs, an opportunity record type condition is being added to the is_managed_service field.
    • This targeted change ensures managed service classification correctly reflects opportunity record type, improving the reliability of partner-facing reporting. (DNA-5711)
  • ECM — intracompany transfer logic
    • Following a review with stakeholders, DNA identified key edge cases in the customer dataset stemming from intracompany account transfers.
    • Logic is being implemented to handle these transfers accurately, with both positive and negative test cases validating the approach. (DNA-5674)
  • Customer360 — D&B corporate hierarchy table
    • A new Dun & Bradstreet hierarchy table is being created to enrich the Customer360 data model with structured corporate relationship data.
    • This table will enable account-level hierarchy analytics and improve parent-child account visibility across downstream models. (DNA-5544)

Why it is happening

  • Partner dashboard work requires targeted enhancements to managed service classification so that opportunity segmentation is accurately reflected in BI reporting.
  • ECM customer data accuracy is critical for Finance & RevOps reporting; intracompany transfers represent a known gap that must be resolved before broader dataset adoption.
  • Incorporating Dun & Bradstreet hierarchy data into Customer360 addresses a long-standing need for standardized corporate relationship modeling across the customer base.

When it will happen

Deploy on

Consumers relying on any changed columns should validate and update downstream references within 30 days of the deploy (2026-08-12).

Downstream impact

  • The is_managed_service field in partner-facing Snowflake models will reflect updated opportunity record type filtering logic.
  • ECM customer models will incorporate intracompany transfer logic, affecting any downstream models that join on customer or account identifiers.
  • A new dnb_hierarchy table will be introduced in the Customer360 layer, available to downstream models consuming corporate account hierarchy data.

Recommended actions

Verify — confirm that is_managed_service values in partner dashboards reflect the expected opportunity record type segmentation after the Aug 12 deploy.

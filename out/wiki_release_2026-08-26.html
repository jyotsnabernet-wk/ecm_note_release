Release Notification

GES Iceberg Transform, ECM Retention &amp; Renewals OBTs, C360 Crosswalk

What is happening

This week's DnA release covers the following Analytics Engineering stories.

  • FedRAMP GES — intermediate JSON-to-Iceberg transform
    • GES data lands in S3 as raw JSON and is not directly usable for downstream Iceberg table creation; an intermediate transform layer is required to parse and reshape the data into a structured format.
    • A Glue Python transform has been built to convert the raw GES JSON in S3 into structured Iceberg tables, enabling sanitized downstream consumption. (DNA-6006)
  • ECM — retention & renewals OBT layer
    • obt_solution_retention, obt_account_retention, and obt_solution_renewals are being created by joining fct_solution_retention, fct_account_retention, and fct_solution_renewals to dim_accounts.
    • Account attributes are being removed from the fact tables so that dimensions live exclusively on the OBT layer; all logic views will be repointed at the OBTs to ensure consistent reporting. (DNA-5816)
  • C360 — crosswalk improvement
    • Analysis of the crosswalk table is underway to identify gaps and implement improvements to entity resolution and matching logic. (DNA-5920)

Why it is happening

Data usability & platform readiness

  • The raw JSON format of GES data in S3 cannot feed Iceberg tables directly; the new intermediate Glue transform provides the structured layer required for reliable downstream pipelines.

Reporting consolidation & model hygiene

  • ECM retention and renewals fact tables carry account attributes that belong in a dimension layer; introducing OBTs centralises those attributes, reduces duplication, and gives Finance and RevOps a single, authoritative surface for reporting.

When it will happen

Deploy on

Consumers relying on any changed columns should validate and update downstream references within 30 days of the deploy (2026-08-26).

Downstream impact

  • obt_solution_retention, obt_account_retention, and obt_solution_renewals become the new authoritative models for ECM retention and renewals reporting, replacing direct references to the underlying fact tables.
  • Logic views previously pointing at fct_solution_retention, fct_account_retention, and fct_solution_renewals will be redirected to the new OBT layer.
  • GES data will be available via structured Iceberg tables produced by the new intermediate Glue transform, unblocking any downstream models that depend on sanitised GES data.

Recommended actions

Verify — ECM reporting consumers and BI tools are referencing obt_solution_retention, obt_account_retention, and obt_solution_renewals rather than the underlying fact tables after deploy.

Verify — downstream pipelines consuming GES data are reading from the new structured Iceberg tables and that the Glue transform job is running successfully in the target environment.

Release Notification

Customer360 D&amp;B hierarchy, FedRAMP parity checks &amp; alerting, product feature-event catalog, Salesforce Chatter ingestion

What is happening

This week's DnA release covers the following Analytics Engineering stories.

  • Customer360 — D&B hierarchy and account spine
    • A new D&B hierarchy table was created for Customer360 to represent parent and child company relationships.
    • The team also investigated whether a semantic view can sit on top of the account spine / crosswalk to simplify customer lookups. (DNA-5544 / DNA-6138)
  • FedRAMP — GES parity checks, alerting and source classification
    • Three rounds of parity checks for GES tables were completed in Snowflake. Each compared row counts by month between table pairs and checked whether differences came from duplicates or missing rows.
    • Splunk alerting was configured for the customer export notebook pipeline. It covers task failures, unexpected data presence in Snowflake, and sanitization errors, with on-call routing defined.
    • Related work is in progress to classify every Overlord source as standardized or custom and flag FedRAMP-regulated sources such as Workday. This feeds the migration wave plan. (DNA-6300 / DNA-6308 / DNA-6310 / DNA-5947 / DNA-6053)
  • Product analytics — feature-event catalog foundation
    • Empty foundational structures were staged for feature-event reporting: a feature event catalog, a bridge of workspace solution to product area, and the classification rules.
    • Each list can live in a macro or seed, and an intermediate model renders it for review. (DNA-6488)
  • Salesforce — Chatter ingestion and sales reporting
    • Salesforce Case and Chatter comment ingestion was extended for the Quote-to-Cash initiative. Sales Operations can now analyze why Opportunities request Deal Desk support and what causes deal friction.
    • In progress: models to compare Bookings are being built, because obt_bookings reflects the latest Salesforce status and reported month-end or quarter-end numbers drift over time.
    • In progress: the refresh cadence of FCT_FORECAST_HISTORY is being moved to Wednesday and Thursday at 5pm Central, with new Account Owner-related fields added. (DNA-6342 / DNA-6463 / DNA-6346)
  • Semantic views — Signals & Actions for support
    • Work is in progress to define and implement Signals & Actions for the sv_support_ticket semantic view, for use with Snowflake Cortex Analyst.
    • The business stakeholders' specification sheet will determine the final list. (DNA-6249)
  • Ingestion platform — Fivetran assessment and Zendesk Knowledge
    • An assessment of current Fivetran entitlement and usage is under way. It covers licensed connectors, spare capacity, and the MAR per-connector cost model.
    • Work is also in progress to extend zendesk.article ingestion with engagement fields (views, comments, subscriptions, upvotes) that the DX team needs. (DNA-6056 / DNA-5171)

Why it is happening

Data trust & compliance

  • FedRAMP parity checks and Splunk alerting confirm regulated data pipelines match existing outputs and that failures or unsanitized data are caught quickly.
  • Source classification and the Fivetran assessment guide migration planning and cost decisions.

Business analytics enablement

  • Customer hierarchy, feature-event classification, Chatter ingestion, and bookings comparison give Finance, Sales Operations, and Product teams more complete, repeatable reporting.
  • Semantic views and the Zendesk engagement fields support self-service analysis for Support and DX.

When it will happen

Deploy on

Consumers relying on any changed columns should validate and update downstream references within 30 days of the deploy (2026-11-04).

Downstream impact

  • Customer360 consumers may see the new D&B hierarchy table and any account spine or crosswalk changes alongside existing customer models.
  • Salesforce Case and Chatter ingestion changes may add new source data for Quote-to-Cash analysis in Salesforce-based models.
  • Bookings reporting built on obt_bookings is the basis for the new comparison models, which will help explain differences against previously reported numbers.
  • Consumers of FCT_FORECAST_HISTORY will see a new refresh schedule once the in-progress change ships.
  • Users of the sv_support_ticket semantic view and zendesk.article may see new Signals, Actions, and engagement fields as in-progress work completes.

Recommended actions

Verify — Customer360 consumers confirm the new D&B hierarchy table returns expected parent and child relationships.

Verify — Salesforce Case and Chatter users confirm the new data is available and expected before relying on it for Quote-to-Cash analysis.

Release Notification

FedRAMP user analytics backfill, GES sanitization, ECM bookings sync, DMF resolution, Zendesk engagement ingestion

What is happening

This week's DnA release covers the following Analytics Engineering stories.

  • FedRAMP — historical user analytics backfill
    • User analytics data was not captured historically, leaving gaps in trend analysis and internal consumer reporting coverage.
    • Backfill logic is being defined to load historical user analytics into Snowflake, ensuring complete longitudinal visibility. (DNA-5902)
  • FedRAMP — GES sanitized Iceberg table & unit tests
    • As part of the FedRAMP Sanitization project, the workiva.common.v0.workiva-event-log topic requires a sanitized Iceberg table before Phase 1 is considered complete.
    • A sanitized Iceberg table will be created, connected to Snowflake, and covered by unit tests to satisfy compliance requirements. (DNA-5622)
  • ECM — bookings and SPP targets team-mapping sync
    • OBT_BOOKINGS and FCT_TARGETS_SPP fall out of sync when teams are absent from BI.TEAM_MAPPING, producing null team and region rows that break ECM attainment reporting.
    • Manual updates to BI.TEAM_MAPPING are currently required as a workaround; this work tracks a durable fix to keep bookings and targets in sync automatically. (DNA-5893)
  • Data Platform — DMF issue resolution
    • Unexpected behavior was observed in Data Monitoring Functions (DMFs), flagged by Data Ops as a potential data quality risk.
    • Investigation was completed and the issue was resolved to restore reliable monitoring function behavior. (DNA-5901)
  • Customer Experience — Zendesk knowledge base engagement ingestion
    • The zendesk.article table in Snowflake is missing key engagement fields — views, comments, subscriptions, and upvotes — that are not currently pulled through Fivetran.
    • Extended ingestion logic will add these fields from the Zendesk API, giving the DX team full visibility into how users interact with knowledge base content. (DNA-5171)

Why it is happening

Compliance & data completeness

  • FedRAMP requirements mandate sanitized, fully tested data pipelines before Phase 1 sign-off, and historical gaps in user analytics undermine the reliability of trend reporting for internal stakeholders.

Operational accuracy & self-service reliability

  • Manual team-mapping updates create fragility in ECM attainment reporting; automating the sync and resolving DMF issues reduces operational toil and improves stakeholder confidence in pipeline data.

When it will happen

Deploy on

Consumers relying on any changed columns should validate and update downstream references within 30 days of the deploy (2026-08-12).

Downstream impact

  • ECM attainment reporting surfaces are affected when OBT_BOOKINGS and FCT_TARGETS_SPP diverge due to missing rows in BI.TEAM_MAPPING.
  • The zendesk.article Snowflake table will gain new engagement columns (views, comments, subscriptions, upvotes) once the extended Fivetran ingestion is complete.
  • FedRAMP-compliant Snowflake external tables sourcing from the sanitized workiva-event-log Iceberg table will become available to internal consumers after Phase 1 completion.

Recommended actions

Verify — ECM attainment dashboards show no null team or region rows after the BI.TEAM_MAPPING sync fix is deployed on 2026-08-12.

Verify — DMF alerts and monitoring checks are firing as expected in Snowflake following the DNA-5901 resolution.

Verify — the zendesk.article table in Snowflake contains the new engagement fields (views, comments, subscriptions, upvotes) once the DNA-5171 ingestion pipeline is activated.

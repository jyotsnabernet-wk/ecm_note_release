Release Notification

ECM bookings, freshness &amp; hierarchy, Salesforce field enrichment, D&amp;B API, FedRAMP security tags

What is happening

This week's DnA release covers the following Analytics Engineering stories.

  • ECM — Parseport bookings integration & renewal model updates
    • Workiva acquired Parseport, and its deal data has been migrated from HubSpot into the standard Salesforce instance; the team is integrating those deals directly into the core bookings model rather than relying on a static UNION query.
    • Renewal model logic is also under active revision to ensure contract-lifecycle accuracy across ECM reporting surfaces.
    • Contract fields are being added to the subscription fact to support contract-related reporting and lay the groundwork for a future contract-grain data model. (DNA-5795 / DNA-6213 / DNA-6117)
  • ECM — Sales hierarchy & attainment mapping
    • Current bookings-to-target mapping relies on a manually maintained GOLD_PROD.BI.TEAM_MAPPING table, which breaks when sales roles or teams change and causes null team/region rows in QuickSight attainment dashboards.
    • A scalable sales hierarchy is being implemented to replace manual overrides by deriving team and region assignments programmatically from the Salesforce org structure. (DNA-5893)
  • ECM — Gold model data freshness
    • Most ECM Gold models currently rebuild only during the nightly run, creating up to 24 hours of data lag and prompting users to bypass governed Gold models and query Silver directly.
    • A dedicated ~2-hour refresh cadence is being introduced for ECM Gold models to close this freshness gap without destabilising the Gold layer or significantly increasing compute costs. (DNA-5617)
  • Salesforce Gold layer — field enrichment
    • The CSRD_Customer checkbox from Salesforce is being added to GOLD_PROD.MARTS.DIM_ACCOUNTS to enable consistent Sustainability customer tracking across Salesforce, Gainsight, and Workiva.
    • The Next_Gen_GRC_Upgrade_Status__c field is being surfaced in the DIM_SOLUTIONS Gold model to support CPX reporting and Controls Management platform-transition tracking.
    • The Private_Account__c field from the Salesforce account object is being propagated to the silver and Gold warehouse layers to eliminate the need for manual Python-based account lists. (DNA-6108 / DNA-6185 / DNA-5908)
  • Customer 360 — D&B Monitoring+ API & data quality
    • A new API client and strategy for Dun & Bradstreet Monitoring+ is being designed to support programmatic registration of DUNS numbers and receipt of company-change alerts.
    • An investigation into the D&B exceptions table is underway to determine how its data can be used to update and improve existing D&B models. (DNA-6044 / DNA-5922)
  • FedRAMP — Security tag replication for IAM event stream
    • Snowflake security tags from the original workiva.iam.v0.realm-domain-event table are being mirrored to the corresponding sanitised Iceberg table to satisfy FedRAMP compliance requirements.
    • Acceptance criteria require that all tags are present and correctly configured on the Iceberg table with no missing or misconfigured entries. (DNA-5841)
  • User Analytics — query performance investigation
    • Queries against silver_prod.user_analytic views are timing out because filters are applied after a full table scan, making even simple queries expensive.
    • A timebox investigation is in progress to identify structural optimisations and determine how to enable efficient cross-event-type analysis. (DNA-6093)

Why it is happening

ECM pipeline completeness & reporting accuracy

  • Integrating Parseport bookings and adding contract fields closes revenue-reporting gaps that emerged from the Workiva acquisition, ensuring Finance and RevOps work from a single governed data source.
  • Implementing a programmatic sales hierarchy removes a persistent source of attainment-reporting failures driven by manual mapping table maintenance.
  • A 2-hour Gold model refresh cadence eliminates the data-lag workaround of querying ungoverned Silver tables directly.

Platform data quality & compliance

  • Propagating Salesforce fields (CSRD, Private Account, Next Gen GRC status) into the Gold layer ensures downstream consumers have accurate, up-to-date account attributes without manual intervention.
  • Replicating security tags to the Iceberg table satisfies FedRAMP compliance obligations and prevents data-access policy gaps in the sanitised environment.
  • The D&B API client and exceptions investigation strengthen the Customer 360 foundation by enabling automated DUNS monitoring and systematic data-quality remediation.

When it will happen

Deploy on

Consumers relying on any changed columns should validate and update downstream references within 30 days of the deploy (2026-09-23).

Downstream impact

  • obt_bookings will include Parseport deal records after integration; downstream QuickSight bookings dashboards and any models joining to obt_bookings should be validated for correct totals.
  • fct_subscription will gain new contract fields; reports and models consuming the subscription fact may surface additional columns and should be checked for schema compatibility.
  • GOLD_PROD.MARTS.DIM_ACCOUNTS will include new CSRD_Customer and Private_Account__c columns; consumers relying on the account dimension should verify no breaking schema changes occur.
  • DIM_SOLUTIONS will expose Next_Gen_GRC_Upgrade_Status__c; CPX and GRC reporting models joining on the solutions dimension will have access to the new transition-status field.
  • The sanitised Iceberg table for workiva.iam.v0.realm-domain-event will have security tags applied; access-control policies dependent on tag configuration should be re-verified post-deploy.
  • silver_prod.user_analytic views may be restructured or replaced following the performance investigation; consumers of those views should monitor for schema or access changes.

Recommended actions

Verify — confirm obt_bookings contains Parseport deal records and that QuickSight bookings reports reflect correct totals after the 2026-09-23 deploy.

Verify — check that GOLD_PROD.MARTS.DIM_ACCOUNTS exposes CSRD_Customer and Private_Account__c and that downstream account-dimension consumers are not broken by the schema additions.

Verify — confirm that security tags on the sanitised Iceberg table for workiva.iam.v0.realm-domain-event match those on the source Snowflake table and that no FedRAMP access-control policies are misconfigured.

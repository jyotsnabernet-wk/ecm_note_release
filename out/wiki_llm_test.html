Release Notification

Amplitude taxonomy, ECM classification &amp; hierarchy, C360 enrichment audit, Utilization facts

What is happening

This week's DnA release covers the following Analytics Engineering stories.

  • Amplitude — curated event taxonomy for product usage reporting
    • Product usage in Amplitude relies on thousands of distinct event_type values; native Event Category is inconsistently populated, so reliable reporting in fct_amplitude_events and downstream marts needs an explicit, maintained taxonomy rather than ad hoc labels.
    • DnA is defining a two-level structure — event_category (broad groupings) and event_sub_category (finer, homogeneity-driven buckets) — delivered as a seed mapping event_type to both fields, joined into fct_amplitude_events (DNA-5446).
  • ECM — reporting classification semantics
    • reporting_classification will keep the same dimension name and value labels to limit downstream churn, but the underlying logic is being replaced with the validated solution motion logic so classifications match how the business expects deals to roll up (DNA-5394).

Why it is happening

Product &amp; customer analytics

  • Raw event_type alone is too granular for executive and product reporting; without a governed middle layer, teams cannot consistently answer product usage questions.
  • Customer and opportunity semantics need to stay aligned with validated business definitions; classification labels that drift in logic create silent metric skew across Finance and GTM surfaces.

Trust, operations &amp; platform

  • Enrichment and rules-based decisions require auditable history so analysts can investigate model drift and retrospective accuracy without relying on ephemeral logs.

When it will happen

Deploy on

Consumers relying on any changed columns should validate and update downstream references within 30 days of the deploy.

Downstream impact

  • fct_amplitude_events and Amplitude-derived marts — expect additive taxonomy columns; dashboards that hard-code event groupings should adopt event_category / event_sub_category.
  • reporting_classification — values stay labeled consistently, but rows may reclassify under solution-motion logic; GTM dashboards filtering on this field should be regression-tested.

Recommended actions

Verify — owners of Sigma / Tableau / QuickSight workbooks over Amplitude facts or GTM classification fields should re-run representative extracts after deploy and compare key metrics week-over-week.

No action required for teams not consuming these surfaces.

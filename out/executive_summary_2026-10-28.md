# DnA Release — October 22-28, 2026 | Executive Summary

- **Finance Retention Reporting** — Retention metrics now exclude Capital Markets and no longer count internal account transfers as churn, giving a truer view of customer renewals.
- **Sales and Pipeline Reporting** — Solution category filters now show ESG and Sustainability as one value, and the Partnerships dashboard now matches the old dashboard's partner results.
- **Product Analytics** — Product usage data now follows one shared standard, making usage reporting more consistent across teams.
- **Data Platform and Compliance** — Canadian production data now flows through the secure data pipeline, with history backfilled for complete reporting.

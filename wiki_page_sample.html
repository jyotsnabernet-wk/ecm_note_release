Release Notification

GTM Metric Definition Changes: $0 Exclusions, ADS Denominator, Reno Split, Cycle Time Metric Removal, updated Reporting Classification logic 

New Workforce & Audit Models

What is happening

This week's DnA release combines merged PRs across two domains.

  • ECM GTM — obt_bookings and obt_partner_bookings receive cumulative updates:
    • $0 first-year S&S bookings are excluded from is_metric_win_rate, is_metric_average_deal_size (DNA-5285 / DNA-5169)
    • ADS denominator is recalculated via a new opportunity_booking_category key (DNA-5169)
    • Reno-classified bookings are now filtered out of existing Win Rate and ADS metrics, with new _including_reno variants added (DNA-5396).
    • Updated New Solution and Reno (under Reporting Classification) logic reclassify upgrades, entity increases, and other upsell activity out of NS and into Reno. Total bookings across the two buckets combined are unchanged.
    • is_metric_cycle_time columns are removed from facts and OBTs (DNA-5421)
    • sv_pipeline gains two new filterable columns: account_name and account_id (DNA-5434).
  • Net-new Workforce Models:
    • dim_workers, fct_utilization_time_entries, fct_workers_availability_quarter, and fct_workers_time_off_daily are deployed to support capacity and time-off reporting (DNA-5358, DNA-5370, DNA-5383).

Why it is happening

ECM GTM 

  • Win rate, ADS, and cycle time metrics were over-counting by including $0 S&S bookings and reno-classified deals that should not factor into standard performance benchmarks
  • These changes align the metric definitions to agreed business logic validated by Corporate Ops.
  • The ADS denominator fix corrects an undercounting issue in the denominator key. 
  • We are removing the static is_metric_cycle_time* flags because precomputed, warehouse level deduplication rules cannot adapt to dynamic BI charting.

When it will happen

Deploy on  

Consumers relying on columns for Reporting Classification, Win Rate, ADS, or Cycle Time should validate and update any downstream references after 2026-07-10.

Downstream impact

  • obt_bookings and obt_partner_bookings: win rate, ADS, and cycle time figures will shift —
    • win rate and ADS increase slightly with $0 exclusion, ADS denominator changes, and reno bookings removed from base metrics.
    • Any QuickSight dashboards or ad-hoc queries consuming is_metric_win_rate, is_metric_average_deal_size, is_metric_cycle_time, or is_metric_booking will reflect updated numbers. 
  • Updated logic for reporting_classification will more accurately classify New Solution and Renegotiation Bookings. 
  • New is_metric_win_rate_including_reno and is_metric_average_deal_size_including_reno columns are available for reno-inclusive views.
  • sv_pipeline: additive only — consumers gain account_name and account_id with no removal of existing columns.
  • New models (dim_workers, fct_utilization_time_entries, fct_workers_availability_quarter, fct_workers_time_off_daily, int_accounts__enhanced_audit) are net-new and have no prior downstream consumers.

Recommended actions

Verify — owners of QuickSight dashboards and reports sourced from obt_bookings or obt_partner_bookings must re-run key GTM metrics (win rate, ADS, cycle time) against the deployed data and confirm numbers align with updated definitions;

No action required for sv_pipeline, workforce, or audit model consumers (additive or net-new releases).

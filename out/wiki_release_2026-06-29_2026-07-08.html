Release Notification

Two-week release: ECM customer data quality & bookings enrichment, User Analytics int layer & dimension, Support OBT & Zendesk ingestion, C360 D&B/ZoomInfo join, dbt docs POC

What is happening

This two-week DnA release (deploying 2026-07-01 and 2026-07-08) covers the following Analytics Engineering stories.

  • ECM — customer data quality & mapping fixes
    • An investigation into grace period logic found that subscriptions are counted as active only strictly between their start and end dates, likely understating customer counts when accounts experience brief lapses before a backdated renewal or new contract is written. (DNA-5512)
    • A separate investigation revealed that intracompany transfers — subscriptions or contracts moving between sibling accounts under the same ultimate parent — are currently misclassified as Churn at the source and New at the destination, overstating both figures. (DNA-5511)
    • The solution_group mapping logic in the customers model has been updated to align with new Operational Reporting requirements, applying revised CASE logic across the SOLUTION_GROUP and BOOKINGS_CATEGORY fields. (DNA-5522)
  • ECM — bookings field enrichment
    • The bookings tables are being extended to include opportunity_creator_id and opportunity_creator_name as they appear in the pipeline view.
    • An opportunity_closed_reason field is also being added to support downstream sales reporting and closed-deal attribution.
    • These additions improve data completeness for ECM pipeline analysis and revenue operations reporting. (DNA-5550)
  • User Analytics — intermediate layer foundation & dimension table
    • Phase 1 of the USER_ANALYTICS pipeline introduces a set of per-table intermediate models (int_user_analytics_*) that isolate individual fragmented legacy sources and standardize their schemas into a single clean layer.
    • These models serve as the foundational tier for the upcoming gold fact table fct_user_analytics_events, reducing direct dependencies on legacy sources and improving pipeline maintainability. (DNA-5519)
    • A new dim_workiva_users dimension model is being created as the single source of truth for user-related attributes across the platform, formalizing the migration away from the legacy workiva_user model. (DNA-5535)
    • Building on DNA-5519, this release also ships 82 additional legacy USER_ANALYTIC tables across 10 new int_user_analytics_* model groups, advancing intermediate layer coverage toward the full 811-table inventory.
    • Each group follows the established int contract (event_key, legacy_table_name, source_event_id, event_timestamp, raw_event_properties); six high-volume tables remain deferred pending Amplitude/User Analytics mapping resolution. (DNA-5534)
  • Support — OBT build & Zendesk Chat ingestion
    • Following completion of Support refinement activities, development of the OBT (One Big Table) and associated IS_METRIC fields is underway, implementing SQL aligned to the end view requested by Support stakeholders. (DNA-5504)
    • The Zendesk Chat: Messaging Tickets and Chat Engagement datasets are being ingested into Snowflake to provide channel-specific, granular messaging metrics that are absent from the existing ticket-level data. (DNA-5173)
  • Zendesk — knowledge base engagement ingestion
    • The zendesk.article ingestion pipeline is being extended to capture engagement fields — views, comments, subscriptions, and upvotes — that are currently absent from both the Redshift and Snowflake versions of the table.
    • These fields are available in the Zendesk Knowledge Base API but are not pulled in through Fivetran, requiring a custom ingestion extension.
    • The DX team needs this data to quantify how users engage with knowledge base content and to inform content strategy. (DNA-5171)
  • Customer 360 — D&B & ZoomInfo account join
    • A new table is being built within the Customer 360 domain to join Dun & Bradstreet and ZoomInfo firmographic data at the account level.
    • This enriched surface will improve account-level segmentation and firmographic coverage for GTM analytics and pipeline reporting. (DNA-5324)
  • Platform Engineering — dbt documentation POC
    • A time-boxed proof-of-concept investigated workarounds for referencing .md documentation files within .sql files, a capability not natively supported by dbt and needed specifically for semantic views.
    • Findings from this investigation will inform future documentation authoring standards for the analytics engineering platform. (DNA-5556)

Why it is happening

ECM data accuracy & operational alignment

  • Current customer models understate active subscription counts and overstate both churn and new-logo figures due to missing grace period logic and misclassified intracompany transfers; the solution_group field additionally required realignment with updated Operational Reporting definitions to preserve consistency across downstream reports.
  • Enriching ECM bookings tables with creator attribution and closed-reason data directly supports sales pipeline accuracy and revenue attribution reporting.

Platform foundations & User Analytics buildout

  • The User Analytics intermediate layer decouples future gold models from fragmented legacy sources, establishing a stable & testable foundation for fct_user_analytics_events.
  • Formalizing dim_workiva_users as a dimension and expanding the int_user_analytics_* layer to 82 additional tables reduces reliance on legacy models, establishes consistent model contracts, and enables reliable downstream KPI tracking and product analytics.
  • The dbt documentation POC addresses a persistent authoring gap — enabling inline documentation for semantic views improves model discoverability and reduces knowledge silos across the engineering team.

GTM & customer analytics enrichment

  • Extending Zendesk knowledge base engagement ingestion and building the C360 D&B/ZoomInfo join table deliver incremental improvements to customer support analytics and account-level firmographic data coverage for GTM teams.
  • Zendesk Chat ingestion unlocks granular messaging-channel metrics previously unavailable to Support analytics consumers.

When it will happen

Deploy 1 on — ECM customer data quality & mapping fixes, User Analytics intermediate layer Phase 1, Support OBT & Zendesk Chat ingestion

Deploy 2 on — ECM bookings field enrichment, User Analytics dimension table & Phase 2, Zendesk KB engagement, C360 D&B/ZoomInfo join, dbt docs POC

Consumers relying on any changed columns should validate and update downstream references within 30 days of the respective deploy date.

Downstream impact

  • ECM: consumers of the customers model will observe updated solution_group values effective 2026-07-01; active customer counts and churn/new-logo metrics may shift in subsequent releases once grace-period and intracompany-transfer fixes are promoted.
  • Reports and models reading ECM bookings tables will have access to new opportunity_creator_id, opportunity_creator_name, and opportunity_closed_reason columns after the 2026-07-08 deploy.
  • User Analytics: models currently reading legacy sources directly should plan migration to the new int_user_analytics_* intermediate layer; models referencing the legacy workiva_user table should begin migrating to dim_workiva_users. The gold table fct_user_analytics_events will be built atop these models in a future phase. Models dependent on the six deferred high-volume tables are unaffected in this release.
  • Support: the new OBT and IS_METRIC fields will become the primary surface for Support stakeholder reporting; Zendesk Chat source tables will be available in Snowflake for downstream Support & CX models once ingestion is complete.
  • Any model or dashboard sourcing zendesk.article will gain views, comments, subscriptions, and upvotes engagement fields once the ingestion extension is live.
  • The new C360 D&B/ZoomInfo join table will be available as an additive source for GTM segmentation, account enrichment, and firmographic coverage models.

Recommended actions

Verify — After the 2026-07-01 deploy, review customers model outputs to confirm solution_group values align with Operational Reporting expectations (DNA-5522); monitor active-customer counts and churn/new-logo trends as the grace-period (DNA-5512) and intracompany-transfer (DNA-5511) investigations conclude. After the 2026-07-08 deploy, confirm dim_workiva_users row counts and user attributes match the legacy workiva_user model; validate new ECM bookings columns (opportunity_creator_id, opportunity_creator_name, opportunity_closed_reason) are populated for recently closed opportunities; and spot-check zendesk.article in both Redshift and Snowflake for the presence of the four new engagement fields.

No action required for — User Analytics intermediate layer (DNA-5519), Zendesk Chat ingestion (DNA-5173), C360 D&B/ZoomInfo join, and dbt docs POC at this time; these are in-progress or fully additive builds with no breaking changes to existing consumer models. Teams not consuming ECM bookings, Zendesk article, or Customer 360 account tables require no action.

DnA Intake Process & Guidelines

The standard operating procedure for submitting, routing, and tracking data requests

About this page

This page outlines the standard operating procedure for submitting, routing, and tracking requests for the Data and Analytics (DnA) team.

What is DnA Intake?

Intake is the "front door" for all data requests, bug reports, and enhancement ideas from our business stakeholders. A structured intake process ensures that every request is automatically routed to the correct functional team and prioritized objectively based on business impact and deadlines.

  • Functional teams covered:
    • Data Engineering
    • Analytics Engineering
    • Business Intelligence (BI)
  • Request types accepted: data requests, bug reports, enhancement ideas

The Previous Process

Historically, requests were heavily fragmented across various Slack channels and direct messages, making it nearly impossible to track status, assess team capacity, or provide stakeholders with visibility into the active queue.

  • Requests scattered across Slack channels and DMs — no single source of truth
  • The previous Jira portal used overly broad categories that did not enable effective routing
  • No objective severity or business impact assessment — priority was subjective

The Updated Process

We have transitioned to a single, consistent capture point for all requests.

  • The "Data and Analytics" Portal
    • All requests must be submitted through the revamped Jira portal
    • Forms use targeted questions — Context, Scope of Work, Acceptance Criteria, OKR — to objectively assess severity (P1–P4) and business impact
  • Automated Triage & Routing
    • Provided context allows pre-approval of critical tasks and proper mapping of tickets to specific Data Products and teams
  • Slack Integration (Coming Soon)
    • A Slack bot is in active development — it will use AI to read support threads and seamlessly draft Jira tickets on your behalf

Release Notification

ECM data quality & bookings enrichment, User Analytics intermediate layer & dimension, Support OBT, Zendesk Chat & KB ingestion, C360 firmographics, dbt docs POC

What is happening

Sprint 6/25–7/8 DnA Analytics Engineering changes. Full logic definitions: DNA Weekly Release Sprint 6/25–7/8.

  • ECM — customer data quality & mapping fixes
    • Grace period: subscriptions counted as active only strictly between start/end dates — understates customer counts during brief lapses before backdated renewals. (DNA-5512)
    • Intracompany transfers: subscriptions moving between sibling accounts are misclassified as Churn at source and New at destination — overstating both figures. (DNA-5511)
    • solution_group mapping in customers updated with revised CASE logic across SOLUTION_GROUP and BOOKINGS_CATEGORY to align with Operational Reporting requirements. (DNA-5522)
  • ECM — bookings field enrichment
    • bookings tables extended with opportunity_creator_id, opportunity_creator_name, and opportunity_closed_reason to support pipeline analysis and closed-deal attribution. (DNA-5550)
  • User Analytics — intermediate layer & dimension
    • Phase 1 (DNA-5519) & Phase 2 (DNA-5534): int_user_analytics_* intermediate models standardize fragmented legacy sources into a single clean layer — Phase 2 ships 82 additional tables across 10 new model groups toward the full 811-table inventory (6 high-volume deferred).
    • New dim_workiva_users dimension formalizes the single source of truth for user attributes, replacing the legacy workiva_user model. (DNA-5535)
  • Support — OBT build & Zendesk Chat ingestion
    • OBT and IS_METRIC fields under active development per Support stakeholder spec. (DNA-5504)
    • Zendesk Chat: Messaging Tickets and Chat Engagement being ingested into Snowflake for granular channel-specific metrics absent from existing ticket-level data. (DNA-5173)
  • Zendesk — knowledge base engagement ingestion
    • zendesk.article extended via custom ingestion (not available through Fivetran) to capture views, comments, subscriptions, and upvotes for DX content strategy analysis. (DNA-5171)
  • Customer 360 — D&B & ZoomInfo account join
    • New table joins Dun & Bradstreet and ZoomInfo firmographic data at account level for GTM segmentation and pipeline reporting. (DNA-5324)
  • Platform Engineering — dbt documentation POC
    • Time-boxed investigation of .md-in-.sql workarounds for semantic view documentation; findings will inform platform authoring standards. (DNA-5556)

Why it is happening

ECM accuracy & operational alignment

  • Grace period and intracompany transfer gaps cause measurable distortion in active customer counts and churn/new-logo metrics; solution_group realignment prevents classification drift between the warehouse and Operational Reporting.
  • Bookings creator and closed-reason fields close attribution gaps needed for revenue operations and sales pipeline analysis.

Platform foundations & coverage

  • The User Analytics intermediate layer and dim_workiva_users decouple future gold models from 800+ fragmented legacy tables, establishing a stable, testable foundation for fct_user_analytics_events.
  • Zendesk Chat and knowledge base engagement ingestion fill measurement gaps for Support and DX teams; the C360 D&B/ZoomInfo join improves firmographic coverage for GTM analytics.

When it will happen

Deploy on for completed items; investigations (DNA-5511, DNA-5512) are in progress and will be promoted in a subsequent release.

Consumers relying on any changed columns should validate downstream references within 30 days (by 2026-08-07).

Downstream impact

  • ECM customers: solution_group and bookings_category values updated per new Operational Reporting logic — spot-check impacted Finance and GTM dashboards post-deploy.
  • ECM bookings: three additive columns (opportunity_creator_id, opportunity_creator_name, opportunity_closed_reason) available post-deploy; no breaking changes.
  • User Analytics: plan migration of legacy workiva_user references to dim_workiva_users; int_user_analytics_* models covering 82 additional tables available for downstream use.
  • Support: OBT and IS_METRIC will replace existing Support reporting surfaces; Zendesk Chat source tables available in Snowflake once ingestion completes.
  • Zendesk KB & C360: additive — zendesk.article gains four engagement fields; D&B/ZoomInfo join table available as a new enrichment source. No existing models break.

Recommended actions

Verify — review customers model solution_group values against Operational Reporting after deploy (DNA-5522); monitor churn and new-logo metrics as grace-period and intracompany-transfer fixes are promoted in a follow-on release.

Migrate — begin updating legacy workiva_user references to dim_workiva_users within the 30-day window (DNA-5535).

No action required for teams not consuming ECM, User Analytics, Support, or Zendesk surfaces — all other changes are additive or internal investigations with no breaking impact.
